Key features that make dental procurement simple

Order from all your suppliers in one place, with intelligent reordering based on your past usage.

Browse and search

Explore products from every supplier in one catalogue and quickly identify the best option for your clinic.

Smart ordering

Get insights into cheaper alternatives and suggestions tailored to your clinic, so every order is simpler and smarter.

Favourites

Heart the items you order most to build quick-reorder lists that make restocking take seconds, not hours.

Budgeting and spend visibility

Track procurement spend, set monthly budgets and stay on target with automated alerts and reports.

Inventory management

Monitor stock levels in real time, get low-inventory alerts and avoid overstocking or understocking from one place.

Order status and delivery tracking

Follow every order from dispatch to delivery with real-time updates, so your whole team knows what is arriving and when.

Multi-user access with roles and approvals

Delegate ordering to your team with custom permissions and approval flows, while the practice keeps full control.
