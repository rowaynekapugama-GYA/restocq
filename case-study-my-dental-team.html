A smarter solution for a growing network

As the group expanded, buying across several sites became harder to manage. Problems with budgeting, ordering and inventory were creating inefficiency and unnecessary expense, and the leadership team needed a way to bring it all under control.

Facing the challenges of rapid growth

Managing consumables, staff time and purchasing across multiple locations created several clear pain points:

Uncontrolled consumable spending

The site was spending $300,000 a year on dental supplies, with little visibility over where it was going.

Manual, time-consuming ordering

Staff spent many hours handling orders, tracking budgets and resolving stock issues across suppliers.

Lack of real-time insights and reporting

Without central visibility it was hard to compare actual usage with budgets and make informed decisions.

Technical support demands

Staff often needed help with orders and technical problems, causing delays and frustration.

Implementing Restocq to drive efficiencies

In 2022, My Dental Team chose Restocq as its procurement solution. One centralised system for ordering, budgeting and inventory changed how the practice managed supplies. Five features contributed most to the savings:

  • Centralised ordering. Staff order from all suppliers in one place.
  • Intelligent reordering. The system tracks usage patterns and suggests reorders, reducing overstocking and understocking.
  • Budgeting and spend visibility. Budgets are set and monitored across locations, with automated alerts.
  • Inventory management. Real-time stock tracking reduces waste, keeps levels balanced and warns the team before items run out.
  • User-friendly interface and support. The platform is easy to use, so staff ask for help less often and operations run more smoothly.

Transforming operations and achieving real savings

Since adopting Restocq, the group has seen major savings and efficiency gains at the featured site.
