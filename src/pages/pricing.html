FAQ

Common questions

How does the free trial work?

Book a demo or sign up and you get 30 days of full access to Restocq. Add your supplier account numbers, place orders and see the savings for yourself before you decide on a plan.

What is the HICAPS corporate rate?

Restocq members get access to a pre-negotiated corporate rate on HICAPS terminal fees. For many practices this saving alone covers the cost of their subscription.

Which plan is right for my practice?

Plus suits a single practice that wants consolidated ordering, favourites and supplier offers. Premium adds contract pricing, up to 4 users, custom ordering lists, reporting, role-based permissions, priority support and Dental4Windows integration. If you are unsure, book a demo and we will recommend one.

Does Restocq integrate with my practice management software?

Premium includes integration with Dental4Windows (D4W) from Centaur Software. Talk to us about other systems you use.

Do I keep my existing supplier accounts and pricing?

Yes. You add your customer account numbers to Restocq and keep ordering from the suppliers you already work with. Restocq simply puts them all in one place and layers on member offers and smarter insights.
