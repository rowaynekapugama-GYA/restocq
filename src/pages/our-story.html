Our story

Built for dentists, by dentists

Restocq was created by Dr Namita Gupta and Dr Stephan Kantharajah, two practising dentists who have walked the same path you have. Having faced the challenges of running multiple practices and the complexity of dental procurement first-hand, they saw an opportunity to make a meaningful difference.

The founders

More than 25 years in dentistry

With over 25 years of experience in the dental industry, our founders have started several practices and currently own three successful clinics in Sydney, the Central Coast and Newcastle.

As both practitioners and entrepreneurs, they understand the difficulties of procurement and of managing resources across multiple locations. The inefficiencies, the lost time and the financial pressure are all familiar to them.

Restocq grew out of these personal experiences, with a clear purpose: to simplify procurement and give dental professionals a genuine competitive advantage.

We created Restocq to close the tech gap in our industry. We want practice owners to have the tools to focus on what matters most: patient care and growing their business.
Dr Namita Gupta, Co-Founder, Restocq
What drives us

Why Restocq is different

Designed by dentists for dentists. Because our founders have lived these needs, the platform is built to solve the real problems dental practices face every day.

Our mission

We combine deep dental industry expertise with modern technology to create a solution that is both effective and easy to use. We are committed to giving practitioners intuitive tools that simplify daily operations, so running a practice is as smooth as possible.

A vision for the future

We aim to change how dental professionals deal with suppliers and manage practice spending. Our solutions streamline processes, lower costs and free up time and resources for practice growth and patient care.

A community of practitioners

Restocq is more than a technology company. We are part of the dental community, and we will support you at every step.

The Restocq difference

Unlike generic software, Restocq was built specifically for the dental industry. Our founders have experienced the challenge of balancing patient care with business operations, and built the platform to make that balance easier. Restocq is a procurement partner that understands the details of running a dental practice, not just another tool.

Empathy at our core

Our success depends on yours. The platform is designed to be intuitive and powerful, so your team can reduce administrative work and focus on patients and practice growth.

Proudly Australian
